1. Project Title -
HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Insights Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze workforce demographics, employee attrition drivers, salary slab distributions, and departmental retention metrics.

3. Short Description -
The HR Analytics Dashboard provides HR managers and executives with deep insights into employee turnover, workforce demographics, and retention patterns. By visualizing attrition metrics across salary slabs, departments, job roles, and experience levels, this dashboard enables data-driven HR strategies to reduce employee turnover and improve retention.

5. Tech Stack -
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
 * Power BI Desktop: Used for layout design, visual structuring, KPI card creation, and dashboard layout.
 * Power Query: Employed for data cleaning, transforming employee records, standardizing job roles, and handling missing values.
 * DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used to calculate critical metrics like Total Employees (1.417K), Active Employees (1.186K), Attrition Count (231), and Attrition Rate (16.3%).
 * Data Modeling: Modeled employee profiles, department hierarchies, and salary brackets to allow interactive filtering and cross-analysis.

4. Features - 
Business Problem & Goal
Employee attrition impacts business continuity, hiring costs, and team productivity. The objective of this dashboard is to pinpoint why employees leave, which departments/salary slabs have the highest churn, and how experience levels correlate with turnover.
Walkthrough of Key Visuals
 * Executive KPI Cards: Quick overview of key workforce metrics — Total Employees (1.417K), Active Employees (1.186K), Attrition Count (231), Attrition Rate (16.3%), Average Age (36.94 years), and Average Experience (7.04 years).
 * Attrition by Department (Donut Chart): Shows the breakdown of 231 attrition cases across various corporate departments.
 * Attrition by Salary Slab (Bar Chart): Analyzes turnover across income brackets (6-10 LPA, 0-3 LPA, 3-6 LPA, 10+ LPA), identifying retention challenges within specific pay scales.
 * Attrition by Job Role Matrix: Cross-analyzes attrition count across specific roles such as Healthcare Representative, Human Resources, Laboratory Technician, Manager, and Manufacturing Director.
 * Age Group & Salary Distribution (Column Chart): Visualizes employee count distribution across income/age brackets (e.g., 472 employees in the 6-10 LPA bracket).
 * Attrition by Gender (Pie Chart): Breaks down employee exit rates by gender — 63.2% (146 Male) vs 36.8% (85 Female).
 * Department-Wise Employee Count (Funnel Chart): Displays workforce headcount across departments — Operations (512), Administration (406), Sales (207), IT (166), Marketing (64), Human Resources (39), and Finance (23).
 * Attrition Trend by Experience (Line Chart): Maps attrition counts against total years of experience to identify critical departure milestones (e.g., high turnover in early career years).
Business Impact & Key Insights
 * Attrition Hotspot: High turnover is observed among employees in the early-to-mid career experience brackets.
 * Gender Breakdown: Male employees account for 63.2% of total attrition.
 * Departmental Concentration: Operations and Administration represent the largest portion of the workforce, making them key focus areas for retention policies
